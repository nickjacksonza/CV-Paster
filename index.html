Career portals ask for your CV again, field by field. CV Paster reads your CV and lays it out as blocks you can copy one at a time: personal details, each role, each date, skills and qualifications.

Drop your CV here, or choose a file

PDF, Word (.docx) or plain text, up to about 10 MB

Or paste the text

Useful if your CV is in a format the reader cannot open, or if you keep it in a notes app.
